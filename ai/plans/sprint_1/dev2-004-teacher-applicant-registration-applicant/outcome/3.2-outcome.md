## Timestamp-exposure approach — DISP-5 disposition (RECORDING OBLIGATION)

**Decision**: `lastAttemptAt` / `cooldownUntil` are exposed as **nullable ISO-8601 UTC strings** per plan-review-R1 **DISP-5** (finding 9): no DateTime scalar exists anywhere in this builder/registry, and introducing one would be a schema-wide surface change (out of scope).

**How the HealthCheck precedent was applied** (`backend/graphql/pothos/shared/health.pothos.ts`, studied first): HealthCheck exposes its timestamp as `timestamp: t.exposeString("timestamp")` — but that works because `HealthCheckReturnType.timestamp` is ALREADY a string produced per call by the service. Our canonical fields are `Date | null`, so a direct `exposeString` does not type-check. Grep for `toISOString` across `backend/graphql/pothos/**` returned ZERO hits — no existing Date-handling precedent exists in the Pothos layer to copy. Resolution within DISP-5's latitude ("mirroring the HealthCheck precedent"): replicate the OUTPUT contract (nullable ISO-8601 UTC string) with a minimal structural field that performs presentation-layer conversion only:

```typescript
// Nullable ISO-8601 UTC string per DISP-5 (source is `Date | null`).
lastAttemptAt: t.field({
  type: "String",
  nullable: true,
  resolve: parent => parent.lastAttemptAt?.toISOString() ?? null,
}),
```

This is explicitly presentation typing only — the canonical `Date | null` TS shape is intentional and unchanged (CF-B of outcome/2.2 honored); the frontend consumes codegen `string | null` into the locale-aware formatter util owned by Task 4.2 (0.2 Adaptation #2). `Date.prototype.toISOString()` is intrinsically UTC/Z-suffixed, matching the HealthCheck timestamp's semantics.
